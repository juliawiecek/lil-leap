Voice assistance

For people who don't use a screen reader: the browser reads out what you move to or point at. If you use a screen reader, leave this off, since your screen reader already does this. Automatic reading starts off. Only installed local voices are offered.

Focus and hover reading require voice assistance. Hover waits for your pointer and skips sensitive account/profile details, and hidden balances are never read. Prices are read once, never on every tick. Escape stops the current reading.

Brief says each item's name and value, such as "Number of shares, 2". Detailed also says what it is and its state, such as button, selected or expanded.@if (access.soundCuesOffered) { Speech speed is shared with the audio briefing.}

Read on demand

Read without continuous focus or hover speech. Use Alt + Shift + R to read an item while focused on it, or Alt + Shift + P for the current page summary. Selecting text also lets Read section read your selection.

Alt + Shift + R: read focused item. Alt + Shift + P: page summary. Alt + Shift + X: stop. Alt + Shift + Space: pause/resume. Alt + Shift + A: Accessibility in Settings, or these tools before sign-in. Reading shortcuts leave typing fields alone; Stop works everywhere.

Visual assistance

Browser zoom and operating-system high contrast also work. Trading palette, chart voice, briefing, and switch-control preferences remain available in Settings.
