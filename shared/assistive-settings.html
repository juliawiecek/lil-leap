Arrow-key navigation

Move around the page with the arrow keys. Alt + Shift + N turns it on or off.

How it works

Turning this on starts at the page heading and turns off automatic switch scanning if it is running. It keeps your voice setting and also works without speech support.

Up/Down and Left/Right move between items in that direction. Enter goes inside a group or starts editing a field or exploring a chart. Escape finishes editing and keeps your value; arrows then move between controls again. A further Escape leaves the group. You can also start editing by typing directly. Enter activates buttons and links; in stock search it chooses the result. Tab and mouse access work as usual. The focused item is highlighted; a dashed outline means you are editing or exploring. Turn on Voice mode below for spoken feedback.

Voice mode

The browser reads out what you move to. Leave this off if you use a screen reader. Alt + Shift + V turns it on or off; Alt + Shift + X stops reading.

Voice options

Brief says each item's name and value. Detailed also says what it is and its state. Hidden balances are never read. Voice mode also speaks during arrow-key navigation when both are on.

@if (access.soundCuesOffered) {

Short sounds when an order fills or is rejected, when switch control moves, and before you are signed out.

}
Visual assistance

Larger text, clearer focus and more contrast. Browser zoom and system high contrast also work.
